Mosque Campaign: 440 conversations at AED 14.60

My cheapest message campaign per conversation, and also the biggest of the three. The numbers come from my own Ads Manager and I compare them with my other message campaigns, not with industry averages.

This campaign brought 73% of the conversations from the three message campaigns for 52% of their budget. Its AED 14.60 is 28% below the blended AED 20.34, and 59% to 62% below the two real estate campaigns.

What I read from it

The biggest budget of the three did not push the cost up here. A bigger budget can raise the cost per result when the audience is used up, but in this sample it did not, and three campaigns are not enough to call that a rule.

The offer and the audience are not comparable with the real estate campaigns, so I would not use the gap between them as a measure of how good any one campaign was. It shows how different the cost per conversation can be between industries on the same platform.

What I would test next

  • Scale in steps. Raise the budget by a fifth to a third at a time and wait for the cost per conversation to settle before the next step, so a rising cost shows up early.
  • Refresh the creative on a schedule. A campaign that runs for weeks on the same ads usually pays for it in cost per result, and fresh creative is the part I can produce fastest myself.
  • Watch frequency and the cost per conversation together, and set a ceiling for the cost beforehand, so the decision to pause or scale is made by numbers and not by feel.
Read this before quoting these numbers. These figures are from my own Ads Manager for one campaign. A conversation is not a sale or a qualified lead, and cost per result depends on the audience, the offer, the creative and how results are counted. Use it as a conversation starter, not as a benchmark.
