Zaora had the smallest budget of the three message campaigns and the highest cost per conversation: 87% above the blended AED 20.34. The other real estate campaign, Salsabeel / Park DS, landed AED 2.58 lower at AED 35.52.

What I read from it

At 43 conversations the sample is small, so a few dirhams of difference between campaigns is not something I would read much into. Cost per result moves a lot at this size.

Both real estate campaigns cost AED 35 to 38 per conversation, well above the Mosque campaign at AED 14.60. In my numbers, real estate conversations cost more, which is what I would expect from a higher-consideration offer. Two campaigns do not make a rule, so I treat it as a starting assumption, not a benchmark.

What I would test next

  • Split the budget across two or three creative angles for the first week, so the data shows which one earns its spend before the budget is committed.
  • Qualify inside the conversation with one opening question about budget, area and timing, so the cost I report is for conversations that can turn into viewings.
  • Track what happens after the conversation. A conversation is not a viewing and a viewing is not a sale, and the cost per sale is the number that matters to the client.
Read this before quoting these numbers. These figures are from my own Ads Manager for one campaign. A conversation is not a sale or a qualified lead, and cost per result depends on the audience, the offer, the creative and how results are counted. Use it as a conversation starter, not as a benchmark.
