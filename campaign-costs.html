What my Meta Ads campaigns really cost

Six real campaigns, two goals, one honest table. These are my own accounts, not industry benchmarks.

The short version

AED 20.34blended cost per conversation, 3 message campaigns
AED 32.73blended cost per lead, 3 lead campaigns
AED 23,536total budget across the six campaigns

Blended means total budget divided by total results, so bigger campaigns weigh more than small ones.

The two real estate campaigns cost AED 35 to 38 per conversation. The Mosque campaign cost AED 14.60, and it also had the biggest budget and the most conversations. In this small sample, the largest budget did not push the cost up, but three campaigns are not enough to call that a rule.

The Carbon Footprint campaign is an outlier: a small AED 483 budget and a very cheap lead. Without it, the other two campaigns average AED 47.20 per lead (AED 10,809 over 229 leads). For planning a new lead budget, the higher figure is the safer starting point, and a result like the cheap one is an upside, not a promise.

Read this before quoting these numbers. The sample is six campaigns from my own work, in different industries, with different offers and creatives. Cost per result depends on the audience, the offer, the creative and how a lead is counted. Use these figures as a starting point for a conversation, not as a benchmark.

What I take from it

Cost per result moves a lot between campaigns even inside the same goal, so I judge each campaign against its own goal and its own offer. The creative is the part I can change fastest, which is why I build the ads, reels and posters myself and track them together with the media buying.
